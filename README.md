# LLB Group Corporate Site

Create a state-of-the-art, investor-grade corporate website for **LLB Group, Inc.**, a wellness consulting, education, and media company headquartered in Chicago.

**Company Details**

* Legal Name: LLB Group, Inc.
* Address: 69 W. Washington Street, Suite 1240, Chicago, IL 60602
* Brand shorthand: LLB
* Positioning: Wellness strategy, human performance, leadership development, and sustainable balance at scale

**Primary Objective**
Design the site to immediately signal credibility, scalability, and investment readiness. The website should feel appropriate for institutional investors, corporate partners, and enterprise clients—not a consumer wellness brand.

**Design Direction**

* Minimalist, modern, editorial, and authoritative
* Typography-driven layout (strong hierarchy, generous spacing)
* Dark neutral color palette (graphite / charcoal / white)
* Subtle, restrained accents only (teal or indigo sparingly)
* No decorative or emotional imagery required at this stage
* The site must look premium and complete even without images
* No “holistic,” spiritual, or lifestyle clichés in visuals or layout

**Tone & Voice**

* Calm, confident, professional, and grounded
* Clear, adult language—no hype, no jargon, no spiritual fluff
* Investor-friendly, consulting-firm tone
* Emphasize structure, frameworks, outcomes, and scale

**Site Architecture (Must Include These Pages)**

1. Home
2. About
3. Consulting
4. Solutions (or Services, if cleaner)
5. Events & Speaking
6. Education
7. Media
8. Shop
9. Book / Contact

**Homepage Requirements**

* Strong value-driven hero section (no inspirational quotes)
* Clear explanation of what LLB Group does and who it serves
* Three core pillars: Consulting, Education, Products & Experiences
* Emphasis on structured methodology, repeatable frameworks, and scalable IP
* Subtle credibility indicators (speaking, media, partnerships—placeholders acceptable)
* Clear calls to action such as “Engage LLB” or “View Solutions”

**Consulting Page**

* Present LLB as a professional services firm
* Include leadership consulting, organizational wellness strategy, and institutional programs
* Focus on problems solved, approach, and outcomes
* No pricing, no checkout, no consumer language

**Events & Speaking**

* Calendar or list of booked dates (read-only)
* Speaking topics
* Booking inquiry form

**Education**

* Structured library for courses, workshops, and digital products
* Designed for scalability and future licensing

**Media**

* Space for press, podcasts, articles, and video
* Clean, curated layout only

**Shop Integration**

* Integrate Shopify as a subordinate module
* Shop should feel like one division, not the core business
* Categories: Supplements, Wellness Products, Merchandise
* Clean, minimal commerce presentation

**Interaction & UX**

* Smooth but restrained transitions
* No flashy animations
* Prioritize clarity, speed, and professionalism

**Important Constraints**

* Do NOT rely on imagery to convey credibility
* Do NOT design like a consumer wellness or lifestyle brand
* Do NOT overuse color or graphics
* Design must feel appropriate for a boardroom presentation

The final result should look like a fundable, scalable company that investors would take seriously within the first 30 seconds.
