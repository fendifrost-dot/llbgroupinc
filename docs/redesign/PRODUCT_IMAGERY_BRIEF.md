# Product imagery brief

The site already has a slot for each image below. A slot stays hidden until
its file exists at the exact path, then the image appears on the next
publish. No code change is needed, so **only add image files**.

## Brand

Warm ivory `#E8DDCF`, blackened plum `#24151C`, antique brass `#B58A4A`.
Headings in Cinzel (uppercase, semibold), body in Montserrat. Elegant and
restrained. Match the mood of `public/images/hero-leaders.jpg`.

## Rules

- No fake testimonials, reviews, star ratings, student counts, outcome
  statistics, or press or partner logos.
- No generated video of a person teaching or presenting. Trailers come from
  the real video pipeline.
- People in lifestyle imagery must read as illustrative. Never label or
  caption them as LLB staff or clients.
- Don't change code, colors, routes, or pricing.
